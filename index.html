Why this match matters

Trust

Officers and young people meet on one pitch, as teammates and neighbours. Sport opens conversations that formal settings do not.

Talent

Footballers, musicians and dancers get a stage. Scouts and coaches are invited to watch and meet the players.

Enterprise

Every contribution builds a team that lasts, and a projects fund for poultry, piggery and livestock that gives ghetto members investment income.

The Day

    Partner with the Team

    Local businesses, institutions and well-wishers can back the squad at the level that suits them. Every partner is given a public appeacition post on the Ghetto Structure socials.

    Give to the Team

    Gifts are voluntary. Nobody is pressured to give, and entry to the match stays FREE.
